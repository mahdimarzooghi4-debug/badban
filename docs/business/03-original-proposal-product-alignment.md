## 1. Product scope

Badban delivers three services under one financial and operational model:

1. **ضمانت وام (Loan Guarantee):** Badban arranges/operates the approved backing and guarantee process. The external lender (for example, an eligible Qard-al-Hasan fund or financial institution) makes the lending decision and disburses the loan. Badban must not be presented as the lender.
2. **تأمین معیشت پایدار / مستمری ماهانه (Sustainable Livelihood / Monthly Pension):** A recurring monthly support service funded from eligible returns generated by investment of the managed principal, subject to approved policies and available realized income.
3. **آینده مالی / حمایت مالی تا سه سال پس از خروج (Future Financial Support / Post-exit Support):** Transitional support after a participant exits the support cycle, for up to three years and only under approved policies. The service is funded from eligible generated returns; principal remains preserved.

The three services are distinct product lifecycles. Shared participant, capital, investment, policy, ledger, identity, audit, and reporting foundations may be reused, but guarantee-specific behavior must not stand in for pension or post-exit support behavior.

## 2. Product-wide business constraints

- The managed principal is preserved and is not used as a source for current living expenses or benefit payouts.
- Service amounts, eligibility, cadence, duration, allocation, and suspension/resumption rules are governed by versioned and approved policies. The software must not invent these values.
- Benefits are limited by eligible, realized returns and applicable policy; forecast investment performance is not payable income.
- Valuation, investment performance, return allocation, guarantee exposure, entitlements, and cash/journal balances must remain distinguishable in records and reporting.
- External price, identity, lender, payment, and other evidence sources are configurable integrations. Until configured and verified, the system must show that no source is connected and must not display fabricated live values.
- Multiple eligible external lenders and multiple supported asset types must be representable through configuration and policy. A lender, asset type, or live source is not assumed to exist merely because the model supports configuration.

## 3. Operating surfaces

Badban has three primary user-facing surfaces:

- **Applicant application:** discover the three services, manage a single financial case, submit/track service requests, review policy disclosures, and view verified case/entitlement history.
- **Admin panel:** role-based access to participant cases, service policies, managed capital and returns, allocation/entitlement operations, guarantee risk and lifecycle, reconciliations, oversight, and audit evidence. Investment/value-engine functions are an administrative capability, not a fourth public service.
- **Lender portal:** handle only lender-facing guarantee cases, disbursement evidence, repayment/status exchange, and reconciliation. It makes clear that lending and disbursement remain the lender's responsibility.

## 4. Current repository alignment review

The existing business foundation and implementation are strongly centered on asset-backed credit and guarantee lifecycle. The repository also has shared primitives for participant ownership, investment/valuation evidence, return allocation buckets, accounting, audit, and exit reconciliation.

However, those shared primitives do not constitute implementation of the three services:

- **Loan Guarantee:** substantial technical foundations and lifecycle code exist; this remains a partial implementation, with external provider activation/evidence still gated.
- **Monthly pension:** the backlog's livelihood/return-allocation concepts are not a recurring participant entitlement lifecycle. A dedicated policy, calculation from eligible realized returns, approval, payable/paid/failed state, history, and reconciliation path is still required.
- **Post-exit support:** the exit/financial-reconciliation foundation does not implement a distinct post-exit service lasting up to three years. Eligibility after exit, policy-bounded duration, periodic entitlement, case status, completion/suspension, and reconciliation need explicit product behavior.

## 5. Required backlog correction

The next technical backlog revision must track separate end-to-end capabilities for:

1. reusable service-product configuration and versioned policy, with explicit type and eligibility;
2. monthly pension entitlement lifecycle, constrained by realized allocable returns and the preserved-principal rule;
3. post-exit support lifecycle, configured for up to three years under approved policy;
4. guarantee lifecycle, preserving the lender-of-record boundary and existing risk/issuer controls;
5. a shared participant financial case that presents all three services without conflating their balances, decisions, or statuses;
6. administration, role permissions, audit, reporting, and lender-facing views scoped to the relevant service.

No production UI, implementation, or status display may imply that a payment provider, lender, price source, investment allocation, or benefit decision is live unless verified evidence exists.

## 6. Decision boundary

This addendum captures the source proposal and the owner's confirmed three-service / three-surface direction. It does not choose unprovided payout formulas, contribution amounts, frequencies beyond the monthly service, eligibility thresholds, post-exit selection criteria, provider contracts, investment instruments, or legal authorities. Those remain explicit policy/business inputs and must be configurable where appropriate.
